- AI is simultaneously overhyped and underhyped. Scammers and the uninformed hyped-up AI while the contrarians and equally uninformed brush it off as a better autocomplete and hallucinating chatbot.
- LLMs excel at enhancing partial knowledge, particularly in technical domains like programming. They are most effective for users with some background in a subject, helping to fill gaps in recall and accelerate tasks at the edges of one's expertise. This capability is especially empowering for generalists, allowing them to leverage their broad but incomplete knowledge across various fields more effectively.
    - If the area of interest is way beyond your knowledge & experience, you've got no way of evaluating the info that comes in, or of coaxing the LLM towards the exact kind of info you need.
